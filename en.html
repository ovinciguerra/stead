Your personal agent, on your PC.

Stead takes care of your email, your calendar, your folders and your errands on the web. It proposes, you approve: nothing goes out without your consent.

Windows and macOS · Android app linked to your PC · English and French

What Stead does

Email and calendar

Gmail, Google Calendar and Outlook: find an email, summarize a thread, draft a reply, schedule a meeting.

On the web

In your Chrome: search, compare, fill in a form. A form is only submitted after you approve its exact fields.

Your folders

Find, sort and rename your documents, only in the folders you open to it. Nothing is deleted without your consent.

On your phone

The Android app talks directly to your PC. Encrypted notifications, reply or approve in one gesture.

What Stead does with your Google data

When you connect a Google account, Stead asks for these permissions, and uses them only for this:

PermissionWhy
Read your emailgmail.readonlyFind, summarize and follow up on your conversations, when you ask or in a routine you created.
Draft and send emailgmail.composeWrite drafts in your Gmail. An email is only sent after you approve it, with a few seconds to cancel.
Manage your eventscalendar.eventsRead your calendar; create or delete an event after you approve it.
See your list of calendarscalendar.calendarlist.readonlyKnow which calendars to look at.

This data stays on your computer. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Full details in the privacy policy.

Your data stays home

  • No Stead server, no Stead account: everything runs on your PC.
  • Your keys and access tokens are encrypted by your operating system (Windows or macOS).
  • Every action is recorded in Activity, and every access can be removed in one click.
